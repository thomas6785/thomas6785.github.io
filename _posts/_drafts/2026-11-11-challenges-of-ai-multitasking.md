Multitasking has never been my strong suit, a weakness that does not sit well in an industry that involves long, long wait times on compile and simulation checks. The explosion of agentic development has only exacerbated this issue, where autonomous AI instances need to be left running for several minutes (or hours) to present their results.

The specifics of how I use AI are not the topic of this post (and indeed probably protected by NDA's with AMD), but I _would_ like to share how I manage the challenge of many parallel workflows.

Talk about:
- tmux backgrounding and foregrounding
- the bell alias
- instructing Claude to make provisional decisions and present them in a report afterwards
- integrating Claude with taskwarrior
- also could probably throw in some pretty relatable anecdotes about kicking off jobs and forgetting (or not caring) what they're about by the time they're done
