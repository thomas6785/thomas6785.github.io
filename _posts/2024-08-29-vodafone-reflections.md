My internship at Vodafone wrapped up on Friday, and I'd like to share the most important lessons I've learned!

**The best way to network is to do a job well.**

Contrived coffee meetings and expo's are great for getting to know your boss(es) -- but my experience at Vodafone taught me that the best way to make meaningful contacts is to work hard and do your job well, and your name will make its way around.

**Work with 1-2 new tools in each project - no more, no less.**

I was fortunate enough to be given my own brand-new self-contained project to work on, and made the mistake of selecting four new tools to work with.
This put me at the bottom of a steep learning curve, and while I'm glad to have new tools in my toolkit, it set the project back a lot and ultimately cost a lot of features I never had time to implement.

**Work in person**

I came into the office almost every single day and I'm glad I could. I was lucky enough to have a manageable commute, and found working in person gave me better focus, and better chances to meet new people around the office.

I had a phenomenal time getting to know my fellow interns and have never seen a group of 30 strangers get along so well -- each and every one was friendly, hard-working, and intelligent. Best of luck out there everyone, and hope to see you all in the future
