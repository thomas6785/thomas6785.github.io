I'm Thomas O'Dea [oh-DEE], a semiconductor engineer from Dublin, Ireland. This site is home to my personal projects, angry rants, and reflections for the purposes of growth.

The site is dominated by discussions on best practices for semiconductor engineering, with particular emphasis on methodology and tooling. The stems from my intense desire to someday see the 'perfect' setup realised for a project. Unfortunately I find myself faced with the choice of designing with bad tools, or being responsible for fixing the bad tools, neither of which would be my preference.
